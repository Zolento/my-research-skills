# Author style profile

Infer only when the user supplies prior writing. Samples describe characteristics,
not sentences to copy or new scientific facts. Store sample digests and descriptive
statistics, no quoted source sentences. Never imitate content. Without samples use
conservative scientific style and say author voice is unknown.

Use [author-style-profile.yaml](../templates/author-style-profile.yaml). Observe
sentence length/variance, paragraph density, directness/hedging, transitions,
citation/figure placement, verbs/nominalization, register, first person/passive,
opening/closing patterns, punctuation and preferred/forbidden patterns. Mechanical
counts are estimates; passive voice and rhetoric need contextual reading. Do not
infer “preferred” from one occurrence, nor treat sample frequency as a mandatory
style objective. Preserve legitimate variation across sections and paper roles.

Explicit user rules always override inferred characteristics and venue preferences,
including H1–H3. An author's frequent semicolons cannot waive the user's H1 rule.
Local awkwardness is not proof the author's whole voice must change. Keep correct
terminology and clear idiosyncrasies. No samples are transmitted to model providers
by the bundled scripts. No writing profile claims to authenticate authorship.
