# Narrative and paragraph diagnosis

Run Scientific Coherence Audit first. Narrative is an expression of an argument,
not a way to substitute for an invalid inference. Diagnose N1 motivation, tension,
gap, contribution hierarchy, claim ordering, introduction progression, method
motivation, evidence hierarchy, result interpretation, discussion and conclusion.

Map paragraph roles before editing sentences. P1 checks topic progression, imbalance,
mini-conclusion repetition, template transitions, symmetry, jumps, redundant setup,
duplicate explanation and information density. Distinguish global author voice from
local awkwardness. A paragraph may only observe, only interpret or only bound a claim.
Do not impose topic → explanation → qualification → implication → mini conclusion
on every paragraph. Asymmetry can be functional and should be preserved.

For a late contribution offer real choices, for example move its problem constraint
to the opening, introduce the unresolved tension before the method, or sharpen the
current opening's final sentence while retaining paragraph order. Describe tradeoffs
in context, not a universal winning template. For a missing scientific bridge, flag
the missing premise rather than invent it as a transition.

C1 makes claims and effect sizes/comparators explicit and puts evidence near the
claim it supports. Existing prior-work delta may become visible. Engineering assembly
must not become new scientific knowledge through adjectives. Preserve statistics,
uncertainty, scope and causal status. Compare abstract and body claims. Unknown closest
prior work needs literature verification, not “novel” inserted from model memory.

Limitations may have disproportionate rhetorical repetition, but remove duplication
only if every substantive boundary remains visible. Scientific importance, not a
reviewer score, controls evidence and contribution ordering. Discuss learning,
alternatives and boundaries rather than repeating result tables. Conclusions may
restate established claims, not introduce an unmeasured mechanism or generality.

Run an independent editorial pass against the source and bank. Decide whether options
are distinct and actionable, preserve accurate simple wording and protect Keep as is.
Do not generate 5–10 issues to meet the display ceiling. Every suggestion is located
and justified. Neither random sentence lengths nor ornate synonyms improve an argument.
