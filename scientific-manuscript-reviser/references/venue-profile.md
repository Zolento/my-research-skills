# Venue and research culture

Accept CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, MICCAI, journal or custom venue labels.
A label alone is not a reliable profile. Use supplied guidelines and representative
writing samples, or permissioned primary-source retrieval, and record provenance.
Without reliable evidence use neutral scientific style and state the fallback.
Do not assert current page limits or policies from memory.

Venue preferences can influence compactness, section balance, methodological
detail, directness, citation density and result presentation. They never change
claims, evidence, scope, uncertainty, terminology or personal hard rules. Do not
make every community sound like generic academic prose. Fit should be grounded
in actual scientific contributions, not the apparent boldness of a thesis.

Architect thesis metadata can say best fit unknown. Never rank a weakly supported
mechanism above an empirically supported thesis because of a conference stereotype.
